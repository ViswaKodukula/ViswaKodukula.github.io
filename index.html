Selected work

Five systems that reached production and stayed there. Each one had a business owner, a budget, and a number attached to it.

SaiDatta Agentics · Healthcare payer ops

Prior-authorization appeal agents

Multi-agent workflow that reads the denial, retrieves the supporting clinical record, drafts the appeal against payer-specific criteria, and routes it to a human reviewer for sign-off. Built with explicit PHI redaction, audit trails and human-in-the-loop checkpoints at every state transition — the compliance design was a precondition, not a retrofit.

16 days → 1–2 daysHIPAA-aware designHuman-in-the-loop
SaiDatta Agentics · Finance operations

Invoice processing agents

Agentic document intelligence for accounts payable: extraction, three-way match, exception triage and escalation. The agents handle the long tail of non-standard formats that rules engines break on, and hand off cleanly when confidence drops below threshold rather than guessing.

70–90% manual effort removedException routingConfidence gating
Lam Research · Semiconductor support

Multilingual support classification at scale

Production LLM pipeline over 1M+ historical support records, spanning 6+ languages and a 38-subtopic taxonomy across 200+ products and 400+ systems. Hybrid LLM plus rule-based validation against a live Synapse product hierarchy eliminated hallucinated entities; parallel execution with crash-safe checkpointing made a weekly refresh cadence possible.

RelPro · B2B data intelligence

Business Summary generation

Tuned and launched the generative model behind a customer-facing product surface, plus the validation toolkit and evaluation harness that made it safe to ship. Quality was the constraint, not throughput — so the QA loop came before the scale-up.

The reference architecture underneath

Most of the above sits on the same spine: Agentic RAG over a vector store, an LLM gateway for multi-model routing with rate limits and safe fallbacks, an evaluation harness gating releases on hallucination and grounding, and observability wired in from day one. Cloud-agnostic by design — built on Azure, portable to AWS and GCP.
